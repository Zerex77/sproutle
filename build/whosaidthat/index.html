How well do you really know them?

A party game for 3 to 10 people who think they know each other. Everyone answers a question in secret. One player has to figure out who said what.

  1. 1Each round one player is the Guesser. Everyone else answers the question on their phone. Nobody sees names.
  2. 2The Guesser reads the answers out loud and matches each one to a person.
  3. 3The Guesser scores for every correct match. Everyone they get wrong scores for fooling them.
