One of you is faking it.

A party game for 3 to 10 people. Everyone gets the secret word except the Faker, who has to bluff their way through a round of clues.

  1. 1Everyone sees the same topic card. All but one player also see the secret word.
  2. 2Take turns giving one-word clues. Too obvious and the Faker learns the word. Too vague and you look like the Faker.
  3. 3Talk it out and vote. Catch the Faker and they still get one guess at the word to steal the round.
