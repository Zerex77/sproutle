How Relay works

Every day there's a word to turn into another word, one letter at a time. The catch: no one can do it alone.

✏️Change one letter
📲Send it to someone new
🏁Reach the goal together
  • Each move changes exactly one letter and has to make a real word. No reusing a word that's already in the chain.
  • You get one move per chain. Then the only way forward is to send the link to someone who hasn't played it yet.
  • The chain shows everyone who moved. The best possible count tells you how many more links it would take with perfect moves.
  • Send the link to as many people as you like. Each one can take it a different way.
  • Finished chains go on today's wall, shortest first. Come back to see how far your chains got.
