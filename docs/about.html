Small games, new every day

playsproutle.com is an independent collection of free games. Most are daily puzzles: everyone gets the same puzzle, a new one arrives at midnight in your time zone, and a game takes a few minutes. There's a word puzzle, a number puzzle, a couple of football games and more, plus one game you can play anytime.

The games

  • Sproutle
    Daily word puzzle · 2 min

    Grow today's seed into a long word one letter at a time, without rearranging.

  • Ranked
    Daily ordering puzzle · 3 min

    Six items, one rule. Put planets, skyscrapers, inventions and more in order in four tries.

  • Trailhead
    Daily word climb · 3 min

    Climb a spring mountain one word at a time, solving a hidden five-letter word at every stop.

  • Keystone
    Daily missing-word puzzle · 3 min

    Find the word that joins both sides of six bridges. Their first letters spell the keystone.

  • Ballpark
    Daily number puzzle · 1 min

    One question, five guesses. Get close enough to the real number and you're in the ballpark.

  • Imposter
    Daily odd-one-out · 2 min

    Five rows of four suspects. Three share a secret. Spot the one that doesn't belong.

  • Gridiron Golf
    Daily NFL trivia · 3 min

    One stat, one pin. Name five NFL players whose career totals land closest to it. Endless mode too.

  • Alma Mater
    Daily NFL puzzle · 2 min

    Eleven starters drawn up in their college colors. Name the NFL team in four guesses. Endless mode too.

  • Critter Collector
    Zoo merge game · play anytime

    Merge chicks into bunnies and lions into elephants, and grow a zoo that earns while you're away.

How the daily games work

  • Everyone plays the same puzzle each day, so you can compare results with friends.
  • Each game has a ? button with its own rules, and a stats button with your record and streaks.
  • Share buttons copy your result without giving away the answer.
  • Your progress saves as you play, so you can close a game and finish it later today.

Leaderboards

When you finish a daily puzzle you can add three initials to that day's top 10. Ties go to whoever finished faster. Posting is optional, and nothing is shared unless you press Post. In Gridiron Golf you can tap a player on the board to see the round they played.

No accounts, no ads

You don't sign up for anything. Your scores, streaks and settings stay in your browser on your device. The privacy page has the details.

Play it like an app

On your phone, open the site and choose Add to Home Screen from your browser's share or menu button. It opens full screen, like an app.

Questions or ideas

Spotted a word that should count, a stat that looks off, or have an idea for a new game? We'd love to hear about it.
