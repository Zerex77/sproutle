Grow a word, one letter at a time

Sproutle is a free daily word game. Everyone gets the same short seed word. Your job is to grow it into a long word by adding one letter at a time, and every step has to be a real word.

ORE→CORE→SCORE→SCORED→SCOURED

How to play

  1. Type your next word: today's word plus one new letter, added anywhere. Then press Grow.
  2. You can't rearrange the letters you already have, and every word must be real.
  3. Keep growing until you reach the target length. Every puzzle is at least 8 letters.
  4. Every word must still be able to grow to the target. A real word that can't bounces back 🟨, and a made-up word bounces back 🟥. You have 5 leaves, and each bounce costs one.
  5. Stuck? A hint shows where a letter fits, and a second hint shows the letter.

Scoring

Every bounce and every hint adds 1 to your score. A perfect bloom is zero: no bounces, no hints. Share your result with friends without spoiling the answer.

A new seed every day

A new puzzle sprouts at midnight in your time zone. Missed a day? Open the Archive from the game to catch up. Your streak counts only the puzzle of the day.

Questions or ideas

Sproutle is an independent game. Spotted a word that should count, or one that shouldn't? We'd love to hear about it.
