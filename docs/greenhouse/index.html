How to play

Swipe (or use the arrow keys) to slide every plant in the greenhouse. When two matching plants bump into each other, they merge and grow into the next plant.

  • After every move a new seed is planted somewhere. Fill the greenhouse with no merges left and the game is over.
  • Weeds 🌾 sprout now and then and take up space. They slide like plants but never merge with them. Push two weeds together to pull them both, or wait them out: each weed withers on its own after 12 moves.
  • Grow a Mighty Oak 🌳 to win, then keep going for a high score.
  • Everyone gets the same seeds in the Daily garden, so scores on the leaderboard are fair. Free play is endless and random.
