How to play

Each bridge needs the missing middle word that joins both sides.

SEAGRASSHOPPER
  • Seagrass and grasshopper. Build all six bridges to win.
  • The first letters of the six answers spell the keystone. Guess it early from the arch to claim it.
  • A wrong guess cracks the bridge. Five cracks and it gives way.
  • Stuck? Reveal a letter. The bridge still counts, but it turns gold instead of green.
