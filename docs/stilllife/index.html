How to play

The leaves in this painting won't sit still. Take a photo and every moving leaf leaves a frozen copy right where it was. Frozen leaves are solid. Use them to guide the dewdrop into the pot.

📷Tap to snap
🍃Leaves freeze
💧Drop the dew
  • Tap the painting (or press Snap, or the space bar) to take a photo. You only have a few frames of film.
  • Moving leaves are only ghosts. The drop falls right through them.
  • Press Drop when you're ready. You can keep snapping while it falls, to catch it at the last second.
  • Thorns pop the drop, and missing the pot makes a splash. Try again as often as you like.
  • Fewest snaps wins. Ties go to fewest tries, then fastest. Everyone gets the same painting each day.
  • Gallery run is endless: paintings get harder, you get 3 lives.
