How to play

Tap a pipe to turn it a quarter turn. Get water from the tank to every sprout.

  • Water flows through every pipe joined to the tank. Wet pipes turn blue and watered sprouts bloom.
  • An open pipe end that isn't joined to another pipe spills, shown in orange. You win when every sprout blooms and nothing spills.
  • Par is the number of taps the puzzle needs. Try to finish at or under it.
