How to play

Tap a patch of soil to turn its channel. Get water from the spring to every flower.

  • Water runs along every channel joined to the spring. Wilted flowers perk up and bloom when the water reaches them.
  • A channel that ends without meeting another one spills into a puddle. You win when every flower blooms and there are no puddles.
  • Par is the number of taps the puzzle needs. Try to finish at or under it.
