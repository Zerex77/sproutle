Privacy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Sproutle is built to need as little of your information as possible.

What stays on your device

Your puzzles, scores, streaks and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and we can't see them. Clearing your browser data erases them.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, names or email addresses.
  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

Leaderboards

If you choose to post a score, we store the three initials you enter, your score, how long the puzzle took, and a random ID created on your device so you can only post once per puzzle. Leaderboard data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Nothing is posted unless you press Post.

Live messages and surprises

While you play, your browser checks in with Supabase about once a minute, and again when you leave the site or switch to another app, so we can send you a message, a screen effect or a power-up. Each check-in sends the same random device ID, the game you have open and, if you've posted a score before, your initials. We keep when you last checked in, how many visits you've made, and the start and end times of each visit with the games you opened. Visit times and delivered messages are deleted after 30 days.

Visitor counts

We may use a privacy-friendly analytics service to count visits and see which pages are popular. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally.

Fonts

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts download.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when optional paid features are added, we'll update this page and the date above.
