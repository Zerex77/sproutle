How to play

Every word in the chain links to the next one to make a compound word or common phrase.

SNOWBALLPARKBENCHsnowball · ballpark · park bench
  • The first and last words are given. Fill in the six links between them.
  • You can only guess a word next to one you already have, so work down from the top or up from the bottom. Tap a dashed word to switch ends.
  • Each hidden word starts with its first letter showing. A wrong guess reveals the next letter and costs a petal.
  • Fewest petals lost wins. A perfect chain loses none.
