      How to play

      Every day there's one stat and one pin for everyone, drawn from 55 categories: passing, rushing, receiving, defense, kicking, punting, returns, and odds and ends like penalties and fumbles. Name five NFL players whose career totals add up as close to the pin as you can get.

      • Each player's number is revealed when you pick them, so you can aim your later picks.
      • Going long counts the same as coming up short. Closest to the pin wins.
      • You get one mulligan: swap out a pick you regret.
      • Any position counts, as long as the player has at least some of the stat. A quarterback's handful of catches can be exactly what you need.
      • Active players count too, using their totals through .
      • Some categories, like penalties or 40-yard catches, only have numbers from 1999 on. The hole card says "since 1999", and players whose careers began earlier count their 1999-on numbers.
      • Want more? Endless mode deals a new random stat and pin every hole, as many as you like.
