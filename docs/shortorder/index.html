You're on the grill. Eight tickets, then you can go home.

  1. Tap the patty box or bacon pack to throw one on the flat-top. Tap it again once it turns green to put it on the bun. Wait too long and it burns.
  2. Toppings come straight from the cold rail. Build bottom to top in the order on the ticket.
  3. Drop fries or rings in the fryer and pull them when they're golden. Salad is on the cold rail. Pour drinks at the fountain.
  4. Hit Order up before the customer gives up. Faster serves earn bigger tips.
  5. From ticket 6 it's rush hour: tickets flip face down after 4 seconds, and peeking costs 2 seconds.

Anything left on the grill stays there between tickets, so cooking ahead pays off. Just don't let it burn. Everyone gets the same eight orders today.
