How to play

Each case has five rows of four suspects. Three of them share something. One is the imposter.

AppleCherryCarrotMango

Apples, cherries and mangoes are fruits. A carrot is a vegetable.

  • You get one pick per row, so think before you tap.
  • Rows get trickier as you go.
  • A new case opens every day at midnight.
